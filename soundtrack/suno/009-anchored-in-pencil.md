# Anchored in Pencil (Four Books) — SUNO READY

> **Source papers:** `SIGIL_COMMITMENT_PROVENANCE_v0_2.tex` + the close of `SIGIL_PHILOSOPHY_v0.tex`.
> A Commitment Record: content-addressed, hybrid SQIsign + Ed25519 signed, hash-chained,
> its digest anchored in the block header's event-log root. It makes a broken promise
> **detectable and dated** — it cannot make the promise be kept. *That distinction is the point.*
> The album closer. The chain keeps four books: philosophy, failures, measurements, wages.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Post-rock at its most luminous, Sigur Rós meets Hammock meets Explosions in the Sky at their gentlest, 62 BPM in 6/8, A major with a suspended fourth that never quite resolves, opens with bowed guitar and a felted piano, glockenspiel and celeste high and sparse, strings enter as a real section not a pad, no drums at all for the first three minutes then brushed kit and a single floor tom heartbeat, male vocal is soft and high and half-falsetto, lyrics sung rather than spoken for the first time in the record, the crescendo is warm rather than violent — layered wordless choir, e-bow guitar, strings climbing in thirds, everything blooming outward instead of hitting, a full minute of pure instrumental resolution after the last word, closing thirty seconds is the piano alone playing the four-chord figure from track 004 slowed to half speed, tying the record shut, the sound of a promise being written down where it cannot be quietly edited

[Movement I — the gap]
[Bowed guitar. Felted piano. Glockenspiel, sparse. No drums.]
[Sung, soft, high:]
We signed the binaries
Every one, forever, offline, in the light
Two hundred ninety-two bytes and a curve
Nobody argues with that

[Strings enter, low]

[Sung:]
But the promises
Oh, the promises floated free
A page can be edited
A post can be deleted
A roadmap can be quietly agreed
To have always said this instead

[Movement II — the record]
[Celeste. Strings climbing. Brushed kit enters, and one floor-tom heartbeat.]
[Sung:]
So we hashed the words themselves
Every letter, every hedge, every date
Signed it twice — the lattice and the curve, and require them both
Chained it to the one before
And put the digest in the header
Where the chain already keeps its events

[Sung, lifting:]
And now a stranger in a year
Can stand up with the block in her hand
And prove that this promise
In these exact words
Existed at this exact height
Signed by this exact key

[Movement III — what it cannot do]
[Pull back. Piano and voice alone. This is the honest verse.]
[Sung, quietly:]
It cannot make me keep it
Let me say that plainly, in the song
It cannot make me keep it
It can only make it obvious
And dated
When I don't

[Sung:]
That's the whole gift
That's the whole gift
Not a chain that forces virtue
Just a room with the lights left on

[Movement IV — four books]
[The bloom. Wordless choir, e-bow, strings in thirds. Warm, not violent.]
[Sung, layered:]
The chain keeps four books
What it believes
What it broke
What it measured
And what it paid

[Sung:]
Supersede — never delete
Supersede — never delete
Write the new one on the next page
And leave the old one where it lies

[Movement V — pencil]
[Everything blooming. One full minute of instrumental resolution.]
[Sung, last words, high and quiet over the wall:]
And the law on the wall
Is a map drawn in pencil
Bring me one honest measurement that disagrees —
And I will hand you the eraser myself

[Instrumental resolution. Strings resolve the suspended fourth — finally.]

[Outro]
[Piano alone. The four-chord figure from Exit Seventy-Eight, at half speed. Twice.]
[Then nothing.]
