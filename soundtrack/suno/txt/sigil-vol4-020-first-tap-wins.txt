SIGIL Soundtrack Vol. IV - The Ledger and the Levee
TRACK 020: First Tap Wins (The Nullifier Stomp)

===== STYLE (paste into Suno Style field, 713 chars) =====
Hard rock in the Black Dog and Rock and Roll mould, 138 BPM, E, the verses are Robert Plant a cappella lines answered by a stop-start unison riff from Jimmy Page and Jonas Hellborg's fretless bass locked together on a twisting 5/4-into-4/4 turnaround, John Bonham drums enormous and swinging with the Rock and Roll intro fill and a Moby Dick-style four-bar drum break before the last chorus, Plant's war-cry wail from Immigrant Song on the chorus, a bass solo break of tapped harmonics and fast fretless runs over drums only, gang-vocal shouts of the hook, Page's guitar solo with the Les Paul on the bridge pickup and a wah, 1971 Zeppelin IV drum room sound, raw, loose, dangerous, live-take energy with no click
