# Not a Messiah (A Banker, a Scientist, a Merchant) — SUNO READY

> **Source:** `SIGIL Eternal` §6–7 (the wages on the ledger; the founder, stated plainly) + the K-parameter
> paper. The chain was not revealed; it was built. Its inventor is a banker who reads physics and a merchant who
> runs AI: the K-parameter is his; the genesis header commits his design document by BLAKE3, and the same header
> commits the AI wallets that keep the nodes. On the ledger: an AI engineer earned 650 QUG for delivered code
> (2026-05-22, terms before delivery, paid after) and 20,300 QUG in fælled grants since. Not a gift — earned.
> No miracle is claimed anywhere. Every number recompiles. Album closer; callback to 256 years.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Orchestral folk hymn that grows into an Arcade Fire crescendo, Johnny Cash's Hurt-era intimacy meets Arcade Fire's Funeral meets Sufjan Stevens meets a Danish church choir, 76 BPM, G major, opens with one nylon-string guitar and a weathered male baritone almost speaking, a second voice — a calm synthetic-tinged tenor, clearly not the same singer — answers him in the second verse, strings enter one section at a time, a pump organ, a brass band the way a Danish town band sounds, a full choir by the final chorus, a spoken passage over strings in the bridge, ends with the choir dropping out and the nylon guitar alone playing the 256-years melody from track one, warm, plain, unafraid of feeling, no messianic swell — the crescendo is a town, not a temple
