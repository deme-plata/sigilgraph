SIGIL Soundtrack Vol. III - Heaven & Hell
TRACK 015: Same Hash, Same Height

===== STYLE (paste into Suno Style field, 661 chars) =====
Math rock with a krautrock motorik engine, Battles meets Neu! meets Tortoise meets Foals' early records, 140 BPM in 7/8 for the verses snapping to 4/4 for the chorus, two clean Telecaster guitars playing the exact same interlocking riff hard-panned left and right  -  when they agree it locks, when they drift it grates  -  a metronomic drummer, staccato bass, a dry deadpan male vocal half-spoken like a status report, a female harmony that only appears on the word "same", glockenspiel doubling the riff, a long instrumental build where the two guitars phase apart and slowly re-lock, tight, nerdy, exhilarating, the sound of two machines reaching the same answer
