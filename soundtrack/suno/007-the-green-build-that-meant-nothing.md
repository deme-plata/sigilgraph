# The Green Build That Meant Nothing — SUNO READY

> **Source papers:** `SIGIL_MEASUREMENT_BOOK_v0.tex` (Part V, the instruments that lied) +
> the Verification-Information-Yield section of `SIGIL_LEGIBILITY_DIVIDEND_v0.tex`.
> **105 passing tests.** An independent probe found **0.4 %** of reads returning data.
> The suite carried **0.0014 bits**. It timed `get()` and never once asserted the value came back.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Post-rock at its coldest, This Will Destroy You meets Have a Nice Life meets Sunn O))) restraint, 58 BPM, C minor, dread-forward and patient, opens with a low synth drone and a heartbeat kick spaced far too far apart, a single piano note struck every four bars with the sustain pedal held until it dies, guitar is present only as feedback and volume-swell — no picking for the first three minutes, spoken word is close, dry, and quietly horrified, a female wordless vocal enters like something reporting good news from very far away and is deliberately mixed too pretty against the drone, drums stay minimal and funereal throughout, one enormous distorted swell at the two-thirds mark that arrives and then simply stops without release, ends on the same drone it started on with the piano note struck once and left to decay for twenty seconds, no resolution, the sound of an instrument confidently reporting a world that is not there
