# Two Hundred Fifty-Six Years — SUNO READY

> **Source:** `SIGIL Eternal` §2 (What "eternal" is allowed to mean). 64 eras × 4 Julian years = 256 years of
> emission; 21,000,000 cap; 154,918 SIGIL minted (0.74 %); 2.96 billion blocks at 0.367 blk/s; 1,755 bytes a
> block → 5.2 TB, one drive. 256 years is 5×10⁻⁸ of the Sun's remaining life. The honest word is not *eternal*.
> It is *unpruned*. Vol. III opener.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Cinematic gospel-electronic, Ludwig Göransson meets London Grammar meets Bon Iver's 22 A Million, 72 BPM, D minor lifting to F major in the chorus, opens on a lone processed female alto over sub-bass drones and a ticking clock sample, tape-saturated piano, a slow four-on-the-floor kick arrives at the first chorus, gospel choir stacked in fifths on the hook, brass swells like Interstellar's organ, spoken male voice in the bridge, huge but restrained, dynamics from whisper to wall and back to whisper, the last chorus drops every instrument except choir and one sustained note, warm, honest, awe without triumph
