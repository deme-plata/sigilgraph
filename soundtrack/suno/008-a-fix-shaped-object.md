# A Fix-Shaped Object — SUNO READY

> **Source paper:** `SIGIL_FAILURE_ATLAS_v0.tex` — 71 real incidents, 10 classes,
> and beneath them the dominant shape: **built-but-not-wired**. The correct primitive exists;
> the live path still calls the insecure one. *The vulnerability was integration, never cryptography.*

## Style (paste into Style field)
Post-rock leaning post-metal, Russian Circles meets Caspian meets ISIS, 88 BPM in 7/8 then resolving to 4/4, B minor drop-D, heavy and mechanical without ever being metal-for-its-own-sake, opens with a clean harmonic figure in odd time over a tom pattern, bass is fuzzed and forward in the mix carrying the main riff, guitars are downtuned and palm-muted in unison stabs answered by ringing open chords, spoken word delivered like an incident report — clipped, factual, no theatre, a huge riff arrives at 90 seconds and is then WITHDRAWN and replaced by the clean figure to make the point that arriving is not shipping, drums are the lead instrument in the middle third with double-kick under half-time crashes, one clean-sung refrain used exactly three times and never elaborated, the final movement locks into 4/4 and simply hammers the same two chords for ninety seconds while the vocal repeats the seven questions, ends abruptly on a dead-stopped downstroke with no cymbal decay
