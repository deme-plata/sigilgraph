## Style (paste into Style field)
Led Zeppelin Kashmir architecture: one ascending chromatic riff in DADGAD repeated like a caravan crossing a desert, 82 BPM, orchestral Mellotron strings doubling the riff, John Bonham drums huge and behind the beat with a phased snare and a booming 26-inch kick, Jimmy Page guitar in open tuning with a bowed-guitar drone under the verses, Robert Plant lead vocal high and mystical with long held wails and Middle-Eastern melisma, and Jonas Hellborg fretless bass as a second lead voice: sliding raga phrases, natural harmonics, a solo bass interlude in 7/8 with konnakol-style vocal percussion, then the full riff returns with strings and brass, the last two minutes are the riff alone growing until it is the whole horizon, 1975 Physical Graffiti production, tape warmth, no modern compression
