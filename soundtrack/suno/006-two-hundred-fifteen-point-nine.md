# Two Hundred Fifteen Point Nine (The Idle Machine) — SUNO READY

> **Source papers:** `FLUX_IDLE_MACHINE_v0.tex` + `SIGIL_LEGIBILITY_DIVIDEND_v0.tex`.
> One bookkeeping defect: cache-restored units skipped the compiler, so they never wrote
> their fingerprint. 19 poisoned roots → 147 downstream units → asking *"did anything change?"*
> cost **215.9 s** instead of **1.0 s**. And then the paper deflates its own headline.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Post-rock with motorik krautrock spine, Mogwai meets 65daysofstatic meets NEU!, 132 BPM locked and mechanical, A minor, opens with a dry unglamorous drum machine playing the same eighth note for thirty seconds like a machine that has forgotten why it started, muted palm-stopped guitar chugging one chord, analog synth bass arpeggio in sixteenths, everything deliberately repetitive and slightly too long — the boredom IS the composition, a detuned piano figure enters and repeats, distorted guitars stack one at a time over four minutes without ever changing chord, spoken word delivered deadpan and administrative, then at the drop everything stops dead for one bar and returns at half tempo, enormous and mournful, with real drums replacing the machine and a string section entering for the first time, final movement is quiet and honest with a lone piano, no triumph, the sound of a machine that produces nothing being switched off and the silence being worth more than the noise
