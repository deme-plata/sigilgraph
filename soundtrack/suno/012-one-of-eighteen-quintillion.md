# One of Eighteen Quintillion — SUNO READY

> **Source:** `SIGIL Eternal` §4 (Why this block, and not one of the others). A 64-bit nonce = 2⁶⁴ ≈ 18.4
> quintillion candidates; live target admits the leading word below 2³⁰ → 2³⁴ ≈ 17.2 billion expected tries;
> network 176.7 MH/s; block interval 2.73 s. Margolus–Levitin: 15 W over one block interval permits
