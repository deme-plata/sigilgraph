# Heaven Is a Spent Thing — SUNO READY

> **Source:** `SIGIL Eternal` §5 (Heaven and hell, defined by the pool) + the coin runbook. The shielded pool
> keeps two sets: 3,288 notes of 32,768 leaves (29,480 free), 1,467 SIGIL locked, 49 registered owners — and
> 59 nullifiers, each a note spent exactly once, forever. First tap wins. A coin is a throwaway wallet holding
> one 32-byte note on a $0.16 NTAG215 sticker; not "secure" — *cash*.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Modern gospel soul, Kanye's Ultralight Beam meets Moses Sumney meets Kirk Franklin with a Blood Orange sheen, 68 BPM, Ab major, Hammond B3 organ with Leslie speaker, a real Sunday-morning choir answering every line in call-and-response, warm Rhodes, fingerpicked gospel bass, claps on two and four, a soaring male lead with churchy melisma, hand percussion, the choir goes a cappella for the bridge, a key change up a whole step into the last chorus, joyful and slightly unhinged, tambourine, it should feel like a congregation finding out that finality is a blessing
