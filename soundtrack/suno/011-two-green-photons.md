# Two Green Photons — SUNO READY

> **Source:** `SIGIL Eternal` §3 (The price of forgetting). Landauer: k_B T ln 2 = 2.87×10⁻²¹ J per bit at 300 K.
> A shielded note is 32 bytes = 256 bits → 7.35×10⁻¹⁹ J to erase ≈ 1.97 photons of green light (532 nm). A 292-byte
> SQIsign signature: 6.71×10⁻¹⁸ J. The whole 256-year record: 1.19×10⁻⁷ J. Physics prices *erasure*, not storage.
> Quillon lost blocks 1–13M to a silent prune. The chain that never prunes is the cheap design.

## Style (paste into Style field)
Minimal neoclassical electronica, Nils Frahm meets Trentemøller meets Ólafur Arnalds, 84 BPM, E minor, felted upright piano recorded so close you hear the hammers, a soft glitch pulse like a Geiger counter, tape hiss kept in the mix on purpose, sub-bass pads that breathe, one male tenor voice intimate and slightly worn, no drums until the second chorus where a dry brushed snare and a deep kick arrive, a single cello doubling the vocal in the bridge, the chorus is small and devastating rather than big, ends on piano and hiss with the last note left ringing until the tape runs out
